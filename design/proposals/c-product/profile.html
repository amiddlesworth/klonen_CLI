About

Head coach at Pacific since 2020, after ten seasons as the Boxers' offensive line coach that began when the program restarted in 2010. Before Pacific he was offensive coordinator and offensive line coach at Lewis & Clark (2006–2009) and spent two seasons at Oberlin as offensive line coach, assistant head coach and recruiting coordinator. He started coaching at West Linn High School in 2002, and was a four-year offensive line standout at Portland State.
