About

Head football coach at Pacific University (Oregon) since 2020. Falconer joined Pacific's staff in 2010, when the program was restarted, and coached the offensive line for ten seasons before succeeding Keith Buckley, now Pacific's athletic director.

Before Pacific he was offensive coordinator and offensive line coach at Lewis & Clark (2006–2009) and an assistant head coach at Oberlin. He played on the offensive line at Portland State.
